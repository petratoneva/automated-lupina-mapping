Research & Projects

My research spans environmental science, geospatial analysis, and biotechnology, combining data-driven methods with interdisciplinary approaches to better understand complex systems and address real-world challenges.

Remote Sensing & Machine Learning for Invasive Species Mapping

Using aerial imagery, satellite data, and machine learning to map the distribution of the highly invasive plant species Lupinus nootkatensis in Iceland.

View the project on GitHub ↗

Wetlands, Biocapacity & Ecological Footprint Accounting

Investigating the addition of wetlands as a land category in national Biocapacity accounts and how their ecological contributions can be quantified.

Environmental Risk Assessment in Greenland

Exploring environmental, social and economic risks associated with mining development through an interdisciplinary assessment.

Intracellular Protein Localisation of MACROD1

Investigating the intracellular localisation of MACROD1, a protein involved in ADP-ribosylation, under cell stress using stable transfection and confocal microscopy.
